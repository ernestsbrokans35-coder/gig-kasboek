Gig Kasboek and your privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Short version: everything you put into Gig Kasboek stays on your own device. There is no Gig Kasboek server, no account, no advertising and no tracking. The person who made the app cannot see your invoices, your email or your numbers.

What the app is

Gig Kasboek is a small bookkeeping app for freelancers. It keeps your invoices, hours, VAT per quarter and expenses in one place. It runs entirely in your browser, at this address or installed on your home screen.

What it stores, and where

  • Your invoice PDFs and the numbers read from them (dates, hours, amounts, VAT, shift lines).
  • The expenses you type in and any receipt photos or PDFs you attach.
  • Your settings: the sender addresses you entered, your VAT start date, which returns you marked as filed.

All of this is saved in your browser's own storage on your device. None of it is sent to the developer or to any server run for this app. If you clear the browser's data for this site, remove the app from your home screen, or press Delete everything in Setup, it is gone.

If you connect Gmail

Connecting Gmail is optional. You can use the app by adding invoice PDFs by hand.

If you do connect, the app asks Google for read-only access to your Gmail (the scope gmail.readonly). It uses that access for one thing only: to search for emails from the sender addresses you entered that carry a PDF, and to download those PDFs to your device so it can read the invoice numbers.

  • It does not read, store or transmit any other email.
  • It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Your browser talks to Google directly. Nothing passes through a server belonging to this app, because there is none.
  • The access key Google hands out is kept on your device, expires after about an hour, and is sent only to Google.
  • No Google data is shared with, sold to or used by anyone else, and none is used for advertising or to train AI models.

Gig Kasboek's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other parties your browser contacts

Loading any website shows your IP address to whoever serves its files. For this app that is:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the app's files.
  • Google Fonts, for the typeface.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare), for the open-source code that reads and merges PDFs and makes backups.
  • Google, if you connect Gmail.

The app itself sets no tracking cookies and includes no analytics.

Your control

  • Disconnect Gmail at any time under Setup. You can also remove the app's access in your Google account under Third-party apps and services.
  • Delete everything on the device under Setup, Start over.
  • Backups you save are ordinary files that you keep; the app does not upload them anywhere.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. The app's code is public, so you can check what it does: github.com/ernestsbrokans35-coder/gig-kasboek. Questions or problems can be raised there under Issues.
